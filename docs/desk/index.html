How to read a proposal

p is the pooled probability from the contest price and the references (weights shown); the band must clear the ask. Kelly is the growth-optimal fraction of equity. Mode prize is the policy pre-registered in report 05 (all-in on the best edge while outside the top 3; it busts in most simulated contests); steady is 0.5× Kelly with a 25% cap. Approving a stake of 0 logs the forecast without trading. Nothing trades without your click. Kill switch: the button at the top. It stops orders in this sandbox only.
